Cathode Ignition & Axial Electric Field (Ez)

A high DC discharge voltage (Vd = +300 V) is applied between the perforated copper Anode at the base of the ceramic channel and the External Hollow Cathode. This establishes a strong axial electric field (Ez, red arrows) pointing out of the nozzle, pulling primary thermionic electrons into the channel.

Governing Stage Equation
Ez = -∇Vd ≈ Vd / Lchannel  (Anode +300V → Cathode 0V)
Primary electrons accelerate upstream toward the positively biased anode ring.
